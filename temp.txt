Web Research Context
KMS 2026-27 refers to the Kharif Marketing Season of 2026-27 in the Indian context of food procurement. The Kharif season typically spans from October to September, aligning with the harvesting of Kharif crops. During KMS, the government procures crops like paddy, millets, and other coarse grains at Minimum Support Prices (MSP) to support farmers. Data related to procurement quantity, MSP rates, number of farmers benefitted, and payments made are tracked and reported through government portals and reports, like those of the Food Corporation of India (FCI) and the Department of Food and Public Distribution. These operations are governed by policies like the Pradhan Mantri Annadata Aay Sanrakshan Abhiyan (PM-AASHA) and guided by MSP recommendations from the Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP).
